# Background Video Instructions

To add a background video to the login page:

1. **Drop your video file** into this `videos/` folder
2. **Rename the file** to one of these supported formats:
   - `login-bg.mp4` (recommended)
   - `login-bg.webm` (alternative)

## Supported Video Formats:
- **MP4** (H.264) - Best compatibility
- **WebM** (VP8/VP9) - Smaller file size

## Video Recommendations:
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or higher
- **Duration**: 10-30 seconds (will loop)
- **File size**: Keep under 10MB for faster loading
- **Content**: Subtle, non-distracting background

## Tips:
- The video will autoplay, loop, and be muted
- If no video is found, the gradient background will show
- Make sure the video doesn't have distracting text or bright colors that interfere with the login form

## Example:
Place your video file here as: `login-bg.mp4`
